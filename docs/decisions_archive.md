- Whether to down-weight snapshots belonging to artists with incomplete history (using artist_undated_work_count, added in the snapshots.py gap-honesty fix) during model training. The naive approach, sample_weight inversely proportional to artist_undated_work_count in model.fit(), was considered and rejected as a default, because missing year is not random, it is concentrated in specific genres (Latin, Reggae, Folk/World/& Country, per the missing-year-by-genre entries above), so down-weighting by completeness would effectively down-weight those genres further during training, compounding the representation problem already documented above rather than fixing it. Four alternatives were considered, in increasing order of complexity, to be chosen from once the modeling stage starts and actual performance can be measured: (1) feed artist_undated_work_count and genre directly as model input features, letting tree-based models (RF/XGBoost) learn any appropriate discount themselves through splits, rather than imposing a hand-chosen weighting formula, simplest and lowest risk of introducing new bias; (2) two-level weighting, first equalizing each genre’s total weight in the loss function so genre representation is not affected by row count or completeness, then within each genre down-weighting only the artists unusually incomplete relative to their own genre’s baseline, separating “this is noise” from “this is an underrepresented genre”; (3) multiple imputation, generating several plausible versions of the undated-work timeline, training and evaluating across all of them, and combining results with standard rules for averaging estimates and uncertainty across imputations, most statistically rigorous but the heaviest to implement, likely out of scope given the project timeline; (4) conformal prediction or similar calibrated uncertainty output, not touching training weights at all, instead reporting calibrated confidence or interval widths per prediction, so artists with less complete history naturally get wider uncertainty bands in the output rather than a pre-decided training discount. Recommendation if this needs to be decided under time pressure: try option 1 first (free, no new bias), and only escalate to option 2 if genre-stratified evaluation at the modeling stage actually shows the incompleteness is hurting performance enough to justify it. Options 3 and 4 are noted for completeness but not recommended given project scope.
